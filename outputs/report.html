Modelling

How the forecast is made

There are three stages. Weather is simulated first. Its rainfall drives the reservoir model, which runs once for every one of the 1,000 weather members, so rainfall uncertainty carries through to storage.

Stage 1 · Weather generator

Rain and temperature per station

  • Wet/dry day: logistic regression on season, yesterday and the last 7 days
  • Rain amount: Gamma distribution fitted to wet days within ±20 days of the date
  • Tmax/Tmin: harmonic climatology plus warming trend, with AR(2) anomalies and a rain-cooling term
logit P(wet_t) = β·[sin/cos(kωt), wet_t−1, f7] rain_t | wet ~ 1 + Gamma(a, θ) T'_t = φ1·T'_t−1 + φ2·T'_t−2 + γ·(wet_t − p̄) + ε
Stage 2 · Inflow

Catchment rain → reservoir inflow

  • Catchment rain blends the point value with its 3-day mean
  • Antecedent precipitation index tracks soil wetness
  • Ridge regression in log space with AR(1) noise
API_t = 0.93·API_t−1 + P_t log1p(I_t) = b·[log1p(I_t−1), log1p(P_t..t−2), log1p(API_t), season] + e_t
Stage 3 · Operation & balance

Release, evaporation, storage

  • Release rule learned from history: follows the monthly rule curve
  • Evaporation from monthly pan rates and a storage–area curve
  • Spill above 102 % of capacity; floor at dead storage
R_t = c0 + c1·R_t−1 + c2·(S_t−1 − Rule_t) E_t = evap(month)·A(S)·10⁻³ S_t = S_t−1 + I_t − R_t − E_t

Backtest: 120 hindcasts per site, September 2016–2025

The models were trained on 2000–2015 and scored on later years. Skill is the error reduction against a baseline: climatology for weather, persistence ("storage stays where it is") for dams. Positive is better.

Temperature and storage forecasts clearly beat their baselines, and 7-day storage error stays under 2 % of capacity. Daily rainfall shows almost no skill past climatology, as expected for a purely statistical model at this range. The next step is to replace the stochastic rain with NWP rainfall (ECMWF, GFS or TMD WRF). The reservoir model already accepts any members × days rain array.

Stations: Chiang Mai, Khon Kaen, Bangkok, Kanchanaburi, Chanthaburi, Surat Thani. Dams: Bhumibol, Sirikit, Ubol Ratana, Srinagarind, Pasak Jolasid, Rajjaprabha. Capacities are normal high-water storage from EGAT and RID. Heavy rain uses the TMD threshold of 35.1 mm/day.
